# Google Merchant Center for Swell

List your products in Google Shopping and free listings, and keep them current. Connect your Merchant Center account once, and your catalog is sent to Google and updated whenever a product, a price or the stock changes.

## Connect step by step

A guided page in your Swell dashboard walks you through the setup: a Google Cloud project, a service account key, access in Merchant Center, and your account ID. Each step shows whether Google accepts it, and what to fix if it does not.

## Your catalog in Google

- Every active product and variant is sent to Google, with its price, images and availability.
- Changes to a product, a variant or the stock follow within minutes.
- **Sync all** sends the whole catalog again whenever you want.
- Hide any product from Google with one switch.

## See what Google says

The Overview page shows how many products are approved, pending or disapproved, the issues Google reports most often, and when the statuses were last checked. Each product has the same status on its own page.

## Know why Google holds your products

When the account itself is the problem, such as an unverified website or missing shipping settings, the Overview page says so, with what to do and a link to the fix. Products waiting for that stay in Pending until you do it.

## A Google tab on every product

Add the details Google asks for, right on the product: brand, condition, category, barcodes and part numbers for each variant, age group and gender. When Google needs a detail such as color or size, pick the variant option that holds it. A preview shows how the product can look in Google Shopping.

## Good to know

- One Merchant Center account and one country per store.
- You create the Google Cloud service account yourself; the app never asks for your Google password.
- If Google stops accepting the connection, the store admins get an email.
- Gift cards, subscription-only products, and products without a price or an image are not sent.
